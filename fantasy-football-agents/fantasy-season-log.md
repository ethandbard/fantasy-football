## League format (for context)

- 8 teams, head-to-head points, **full PPR** (1 point per reception)
- Starters: 1 QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, 1 TE, 2 FLEX (RB/WR/TE), 1 D/ST, 1 K
- Bench: 6, plus 1 IR slot (ESPN only allows players tagged IR/Out into the IR slot)
- Lineups lock individually at each player's game time
- Waivers: 1-day period, order resets weekly to inverse standings, process Wednesday morning; no acquisition limit
- Trade deadline: Dec 2, 2026; playoffs: 4 teams; trade review 1 day, 2 votes to veto
- Because it's an 8-team league, the free-agent pool is deep — spare QBs and streaming D/STs are always available. Carrying more than one backup QB is wasted roster space. Players who'd be "waiver wire headliners" in 12-team leagues are often sitting free here.

### Working method (for whoever runs this next)
1. Read the team page for last week's actual points and this week's opponent.
2. Don't lean on ESPN projections alone. Check usage (snap %, targets, carries, routes) and news: FantasyPros, NFL.com, CBS, Yahoo, NBC/Rotoworld, RotoWire player notes, team beat sites. Reddit r/fantasyfootball is readable through the user's Chrome tab (old.reddit.com), not from the sandbox (blocked).
3. Scan waivers by position sorted by last week's FPTS; compare to bench.
4. Queue claims in priority order with fallbacks sharing the same drop player; confirm add/drops with Ethan first.
5. Set the lineup; verify on the page; update this log.
